### RECITALS:

**WHEREAS**, the Technology Provider has developed a smart respiratory clinical assessment and decision-support web application ("**Health Vibe AI Platform**") incorporating deterministic respiratory triage routing, vital signs intake, multi-assessment longitudinal comparisons, doctor verification queues, and cryptographically sealed clinical reporting;

**WHEREAS**, the Clinical Partner operates an outpatient medical facility specializing in pulmonary and respiratory healthcare and employs licensed medical practitioners;

**WHEREAS**, the Parties desire to collaborate in a structured, non-commercial clinical pilot to evaluate the usability, clinical workflow compatibility, turnaround efficiency, and decision-support accuracy of the Health Vibe AI Platform across **one (1) medical clinic** and **three (3) participating licensed physicians** over a defined period of **thirty (30) days**;

**NOW, THEREFORE**, the Parties set forth their mutual understanding and intentions as follows:

---

### 1. Scope & Objectives of the Pilot
1.1. **Pilot Scope:** The clinical pilot shall be conducted exclusively at the Clinical Partner's designated facility located at `[Clinic Address]` ("**Pilot Facility**").  
1.2. **Participating Clinicians:** The pilot shall involve three (3) designated licensed pulmonologists/physicians:
- **Physician 1 (Lead Pulmonologist & Medical Director):** `[Dr. Lead Name, MD - License #]`
- **Physician 2 (Attending Pulmonologist):** `[Dr. Attending Name, MD - License #]`
- **Physician 3 (Specialist Pulmonologist):** `[Dr. Specialist Name, MD - License #]`  
1.3. **Evaluation Objectives:**
- Assess the integration of digital respiratory triage intake into the clinic's outpatient workflow.
- Measure reduction in physician administrative turnaround time for issuing certified clinical reports.
- Evaluate clinical concordance between deterministic rule-based priority suggestions and attending physician decisions.
- Verify patient adherence to doctor-scheduled reassessment reminders.

---

### 2. Legal Status: Non-Binding Intent with Binding Operational Clauses
2.1. **Non-Binding Nature:** With the exception of Sections 3 (Confidentiality & IP), 4 (Clinical Responsibility & Medical Liability), 5 (Data Privacy & Security), and 7 (Termination & Stopping Protocol), this Letter of Intent represents a statement of mutual collaborative intent and does not create an obligation to enter into any future commercial contract, procurement agreement, or license purchase.  
2.2. **Zero Financial Consideration:** The pilot shall be conducted at **zero software cost** to the Clinical Partner. Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any expenses incurred in participating in the pilot.

---

### 3. Confidentiality & Intellectual Property (Binding)
3.1. **Confidential Information:** Each Party agrees that all technical, clinical, algorithmic, operational, and financial information disclosed in connection with this pilot shall be maintained in strict confidence and shall not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent.  
3.2. **Technology Ownership:** Health Vibe AI retains all worldwide right, title, and interest in and to the platform, source code, deterministic rules engine, user interfaces, algorithms, and documentation. No intellectual property rights are transferred to the Clinical Partner.  
3.3. **Clinical Feedback Rights:** Technology Provider is granted a non-exclusive, royalty-free, perpetual license to use de-identified, aggregated feedback, workflow metrics, and feature suggestions to improve the platform.

---

### 4. Clinical Responsibility & Medical Liability (Binding)
4.1. **Physician Independence:** The Parties explicitly agree that the Health Vibe AI Platform is an **administrative and decision-support tool only**. It does **NOT** practice medicine, does **NOT** provide autonomous medical diagnoses, and does **NOT** prescribe medical treatments.  
4.2. **Attending Physician Sole Authority:** The Clinical Partner and its licensed attending physicians retain 100% independent clinical authority, judgment, and responsibility for patient examination, differential diagnosis, diagnostic orders, prescription regimens, and therapeutic management.  
4.3. **Mandatory Doctor Sign-Off:** No medical report or diagnostic result generated through the platform shall be issued or accessible to a patient without the explicit, digital signature and clinical verification of a licensed attending physician.

---

### 5. Data Privacy, Patient Consent & Security (Binding)
5.1. **Regulatory Compliance:** The Parties shall process all patient information in strict compliance with the **Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020)**, the Medical Code of Ethics, and international standards (HIPAA and GDPR privacy principles).  
5.2. **Patient Informed Consent:** No patient data shall be entered into the platform without the patient's (or legal guardian's) prior explicit digital consent covering data processing, clinical assessment review, and AI advisory notice.  
5.3. **Medical Records Ownership:** The Clinical Partner retains full ownership of all patient medical records and diagnostic files. Technology Provider acts strictly as a secure data processor under documented multi-tenant isolation.  
5.4. **Data Security & Isolation:** Technology Provider warrants that multi-tenant isolation (`clinicId` scoping) is enforced, preventing any unauthorized cross-clinic access or PHI leakage. Support personnel access to vital signs and clinical diagnoses shall remain cryptographically and procedurally restricted.

---

### 6. Operational Commitments & Resources
6.1. **Technology Provider Commitments:**
- Provision of four (4) authenticated user accounts (3 doctors, 1 clinic administrator) within an isolated tenant environment.
- Delivery of comprehensive staff training modules (Module 1: Reception, Module 2: Nursing, Module 3: Physicians).
- Provision of Tier-1 and Tier-2 operational technical support throughout the pilot duration.
6.2. **Clinical Partner Commitments:**
- Ensure participating physicians complete the 120-minute onboarding curriculum before patient enrollment.
- Make certified pulse oximeter hardware available at the clinic intake desk.
- Provide weekly qualitative clinical feedback using the standard Feedback Registry.

---

### 7. Term, Termination & Immediate Safety Suspension (Binding)
7.1. **Term:** The pilot shall commence on `[Agreed Pilot Kickoff Date]` and run for thirty (30) consecutive calendar days unless terminated earlier or extended by mutual written agreement.  
7.2. **Immediate Circuit Breaker (Safety Suspension):** Either Party, including Doctor 1 (Medical Director), has the unilateral right to **immediately suspend or terminate the pilot with zero penalty** upon the occurrence of:
- Any clinical misrouting of an acute hypoxemia or emergency patient;
- Any breach of patient data privacy or cross-tenant security;
- Persistent technical instability exceeding 60 minutes during clinic hours;
- A formal clinical safety objection raised in writing by participating physicians.  
7.3. **Data Return & Deletion:** Upon pilot completion or termination, all clinical records shall be exported to the Clinical Partner in standard format, and test instances shall be decommissioned in accordance with the documented Data Lifecycle Policy.

---

### 8. Non-Fabrication & Ethical Conduct Warranty
The Parties warrant that:
- No approvals, clinical outcomes, doctor credentials, or patient records have been or will be fabricated, falsified, or simulated as genuine.
- Neither Party will contact, register, or represent external regulatory bodies or healthcare syndicates without explicit prior written authorization.
